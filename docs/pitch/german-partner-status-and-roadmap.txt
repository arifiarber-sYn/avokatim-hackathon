3. WHAT REMAINS FOR THE NEXT PHASE (DEFERRED BY DECISION)
==================================================================

3.1 beA INTEGRATION — the single biggest remaining barrier
    What it is: the mandatory electronic mailbox of the German bar
    (besonderes elektronisches Anwaltspostfach). Without it, a German
    lawyer still needs a second tool for court communication; with it,
    we are a complete replacement for the incumbents.
    What it requires — and why this is partner territory:
    - A beA card. It is only issued to lawyers admitted in Germany
      (via the Zertifizierungsstelle der Bundesnotarkammer; the basic
      card costs roughly 50 EUR per year, a signature-capable card
      somewhat more). We cannot obtain one — you can.
    - Access to the software interface (the KSW interface that
      Kanzleisoftware vendors use), which involves an agreement with
      BRAK's operator. Applying for this is far more credible with a
      German Rechtsanwalt / German entity on the application.
    - Real-mailbox testing: sending and receiving through your actual
      beA account, empfangsbekenntnis handling, court submission
      formats (XJustiz). Weeks of joint testing, not a one-day task.
    Our side is prepared: the document pipeline, matter linkage and
    audit trail that beA messages will flow into already exist.

3.2 LOCAL LLM FINE-TUNING — from "private" to "specialist"
    Where we are today: German requests are already answered by a
    local model (Gemma 3 27B) running on our own GPU (RTX 5090,
    32 GB VRAM, ~50 tokens/second). No German client data needs to
    leave our infrastructure even for AI answers.
    What the next phase adds: fine-tuning that model on German legal
    language and our corpus (statutes, 16k+ decisions and growing,
    pleading structures), plus a dedicated inference server so the
    lawyer-facing latency stays constant as offices are added. The
    hardware sizing is specified; this is a planned investment, not
    a research question.
    Why it matters commercially: "our AI runs in-house and is trained
    for German law" is a sales argument none of the incumbents can
    match at our price point — RA-MICRO's JURA KI still calls an
    external provider.

3.3 EDA / MAHNVERFAHREN END-TO-END CERTIFICATION
    The EDA file we generate is validated against the public
    specification ("Bedingungen fuer die maschinelle Bearbeitung")
    and marked Beta. What is missing is one real submission to a
    Mahngericht through an EGVP/beA client to confirm acceptance
    end-to-end. This needs a real German creditor case — ideally one
    of yours. One successful Mahnbescheid and the Beta label comes off.

4. CONCRETE PARTNER ACTIONS (WITH THE REAL COSTS)
==================================================================

These are deliberately small, cheap steps — but each one unlocks a
finished feature, and each one requires a German presence:

1. SIP TRUNK + GERMAN PHONE NUMBER (unlocks the phone receptionist).
   A sipgate business account with one German number costs roughly
   10-20 EUR per month plus cents per minute. We enter the SIP
   credentials in the configuration and Ava answers calls the same
   day. The entire voice stack is already built and tested.

2. beA CARD + INTERFACE APPLICATION (unlocks court communication).
   Your beA card (~50 EUR/year, BNotK) and your name on the interface
   application to BRAK. Development is our side; testing against your
   real mailbox is joint work in the next phase.

3. FIRST REAL MAHNBESCHEID (removes the Beta label from EDA).
   Take one overdue receivable, generate the EDA file from the
   platform, submit it through your EGVP/beA access. Court fee for a
   Mahnbescheid starts at 36 EUR (recoverable from the debtor).

4. MICROSOFT 365 SIDELOAD (verifies the Outlook add-in in the field).
   Any real M365 mailbox (Business Basic is ~6 EUR/user/month, or an
   existing one) to sideload the add-in manifest and confirm the
   one-click "save to matter" flow in a genuine Kanzlei inbox.

5. NOTHING TO PAY: the openlegaldata dump (instance courts — LG, OLG,
   AG decisions) is a free download; once placed on the server, the
   nightly import extends the corpus from federal courts to the
   courts where day-to-day cases are actually decided.
