Kosovo publishes public procurement in three disconnected places: the OCDS registry of tenders and awards (9,063 records for 2025), the Procurement Review Body's decisions on complaints (scanned PDFs with no text layer), and the procurement law itself (04/L-042). Answering one practical question - who won a contract, was it challenged, and on what legal ground - means three systems, manual PDF reading, and hours of work.

We built a frozen corpus (56 MB, 53 SHA-256 hashes) and an offline retrieval-and-citation agent that joins the three sources on the procurement number. A Review Body decision cites 622-25-1796-2-2-1; the agent finds that exact record in the OCDS registry (Dezinsektimi i Prizrenit, EUR 80,000) and the law articles the panel cites (105, 106, 117). Every answer carries the document, the quote and the identifier. When the corpus holds no evidence, the agent says "I don't know" with the reason instead of inventing an answer.

Reproducible without a database, an API key or a network: make demo-offline. Seven contract tests verify the hashes, the cross-source join and the refusal path. Scanned decisions were OCR'd with Tesseract (Albanian); the duplicate file served by the source site is deduplicated by content hash.
